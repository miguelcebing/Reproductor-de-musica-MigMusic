# ADR-006: Free-tier platform verification (Vercel · Render · Neon)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Related:** `DEPLOY-001`, `DEPLOY-005`, `CONS-005`, `ADR-003`

## Context

`deployment-cloud` requires checking the *current* documentation of the chosen
platforms before deploying: prices, limits, regions and sleep policies change
often. Verified against official docs on **2026-09-29**.

## Verified facts

### Render — backend, Free plan

- **Cost:** $0 — **750 instance-hours per workspace per calendar month**
  (reset monthly, no rollover; spun-down services do not consume hours).
- **Resources:** 512 MB RAM, 0.1 CPU.
- **Sleep policy:** spins down after **15 min** without inbound HTTP/WebSocket
  traffic; cold start ≈ **1 min**. Mitigated by the UptimeRobot ping every
  5 min against `/api/health` (see `ADR-003`).
- **Exhaustion:** used-up hours or outbound bandwidth (no payment method
  attached) suspends the free services until the next month.
- **Region:** chosen when the service is created.
- **Secrets:** environment panel only; `SESSION_SECRET_KEY` is generated by the
  blueprint (`generateValue`) and never stored in the repository.

### Vercel — frontend, Hobby plan

- **Cost:** $0 — 100 GB Fast Data Transfer/month, 100 deployments/day,
  automatic HTTPS, preview deployment per push.
- **Proxy:** *proxied request timeout* is **120 s**, which bounds the `/api/*`
  rewrite to Render (long polling is not used).
- **Edge:** static assets served from the global edge; functions default to
  region `iad1` (not used by the rewrite proxy).
- **Exceeding caps:** the feature pauses until the 30-day window resets.

### Neon — PostgreSQL, Free plan

- **Cost:** $0 — **0.5 GB storage per project**, **100 CU-hours/month** of
  compute (1 CU ≈ 4 GB RAM), autoscaling up to 2 CU, 10 branches, 5 GB egress.
- **Sleep policy:** compute **scales to zero after 5 min** of inactivity
  (cannot be disabled on Free) and resumes in ~100 ms on the next query.
- **Exhaustion:** compute/egress limits suspend compute until the next period
  (data is preserved); exceeding the storage cap blocks writes that grow it.
- **Region:** selected at project creation in the console.

## Decision

Stay on the free tiers for the demo (`CONS-005 = presupuesto 0 $`). Accepted
trade-offs: cold starts (Render ≈ 1 min, Neon auto-resume) and monthly usage
caps. First paid step if traffic grows: Render Starter ($7/mo) and/or Neon
Launch (pay-as-you-go).

## Consequences

- All three tiers stay within budget; the app is a demo, not 24/7 production.
- Logs must never contain tokens, cookies or the Client Secret (`F1-LOG`); the
  interactive API docs are disabled under `APP_ENV=production`.
- If a free tier suspends the service mid-month, the smoke-test checklist in
  `SKILL7` documents how to verify a recovery deploy.
